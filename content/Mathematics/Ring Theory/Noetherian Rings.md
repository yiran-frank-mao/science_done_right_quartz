> [!definition] Noetherian Ring
> For (possibly non-commutative) ring $R$, we say $R$ is *left noetherian* if every left ideal of $R$ is finitely generated, and *right noetherian* if every right ideal of $R$ is finitely generated. If $R$ is both left and right noetherian, we say $R$ is *noetherian*.
> Equivalently, we can formulate the definition in terms of the *ascending chain condition (ACC)*. That is, $R$ is left (right) noetherian if every ascending chain of left (right) ideals $I_{1}\subseteq I_{2} \subseteq \cdots$ stabilizes eventually.
> 

> [!remark]
> For commutative rings, the notions of left and right noetherian coincide.
>
