In [[Factorization]], we talked about Euclidean domains and unique factorization domains. In this section, we introduce another class of integral domains called *Bézout domains*. As the name suggests, Bézout domains are integral domains where the [[Division and Prime#^a2ecfb|Bézout identity]] holds. They are also a generalization of principal ideal domains.

> [!definition] Bézout Domain
> A *Bézout domain* is an [[Ring, Field and Integral Domain#^domain|integral domain]] $R$ such that the sum of any two principal ideals is again a principal ideal.
>
