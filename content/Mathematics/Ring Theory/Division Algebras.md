> [!definition] Division Algebra
> A *division algebra* over a field $F$ is an associative $F$-algebra $D$ with $1\neq 0$ such that every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse. 
> 

<u><b>e.g.</b></u>  
- A field is simply a commutative division algebra;
- $\C$ is a division algebra over $\R$, with dimension 2;
- The quaternions $\mathbb{H}$ is a 4-dimensional division algebra over $\R$.
$\quad$

> [!theorem]
> Every finite-dimensional division algebra over an algebraically closed field is just that field itself.
> 

> [!theorem] Frobenius Theorem
> Every finite-dimensional division algebra over $\R$ is isomorphic to $\R$, $\C$ or $\mathbb{H}$. 
>
