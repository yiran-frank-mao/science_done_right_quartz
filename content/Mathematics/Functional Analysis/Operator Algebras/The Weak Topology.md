> [!definition] Weak Topology
> Suppose $X$ is a [[Mathematics/Functional Analysis/Topological Vector Spaces/Topological Vector Spaces#^dd5802|topological vector space]], the *weak topology* on $X$, denoted $\sigma(X,X^{*})$, is the locally convex topology generated by the seminorms $\{x\mapsto |f(x)| : f\in X^{*} \}$. In other words, it is the [[Constructions on Topological Spaces#^concept-895d850bc290|initial topology]] on $X$ induced by the family of continuous linear functionals $X^{*}$.
> 

## Weak\* Topology

> [!definition] Polar
> 
> 


> [!theorem] Alaoglu Theorem
> Let $X$ be a topological vector space that is $T_{1}$, $V$ be a neighborhood of $0$ in $X$, define 
> $$ K:=\{\varphi \in X^{*} \mid |\varphi(x)|\leq 1 \text{ for all } x\in V \}. $$
> Then $K$ is compact in the weak\* topology on $X^{*}$.

### Applications of Alaoglu Theorem

> [!theorem] Bipolar Theorem
> 

The following theorem can be seen as the generalization of the [[The Baire Category Theorem#^9c4a11|uniform boundedness principle]]:

> [!theorem]
> Let $X$ be a $T_{1}$ locally convex topological vector space, then a subset $A$ is bounded iff it is weakly bounded.
> 

> [!corollary]
> Let $X$ be a normed space,  and $E\subset X$ satisfies that $\sup_{x\in E}|\varphi(x)|<\infty$ for all $\varphi\in X^{*}$, then there exists $M>0$ such that $\|x\|\leq M$ for all $x\in E$.
