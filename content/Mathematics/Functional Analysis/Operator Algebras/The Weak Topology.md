> [!definition] Weak Topology
> Suppose $X$ is a [[Mathematics/Functional Analysis/Topological Vector Spaces/Topological Vector Spaces#^dd5802|topological vector space]], the *weak topology* on $X$, denoted $\sigma(X,X^{*})$, is the locally convex topology generated by the seminorms $\{x\mapsto |f(x)| : f\in X^{*} \}$. In other words, it is the initial topology on $X$ induced by the family of continuous linear functionals $X^{*}$.
>
