## Jones Relation

> [!definition] Temperley–Lieb Algebra
> Suppose $R$ is a commutative ring and fix some $\delta\in R$. The *Temperley–Lieb algebra* $\newcommand{TL}{\mathrm{TL}}\TL_{n}(\tau)$ is a unital associative algebra over $R$ generated by $1=e_{0}, e_1, e_2, \dots, e_{n-1}$ subject to the *Jones relations*:
> - $e_{i}^{2} = \delta e_{i}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-1$;
> - $e_{i}e_{i+1}e_{i}=e_{i}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-2$;
> - $e_{i+1}e_{i}e_{i+1}=e_{i+1}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-2$;
> - $e_{i}e_{j}=e_{j}e_{i}$ for $|i-j|\geq 2$.
> $\quad$

## Kauffman’s Presentation

The Temperley–Lieb algebra $\TL_{n}(\tau)$ can be nicely represented graphically using planar diagrams. Each generator can be visualized as a rectangular [[Planar Graphs#^c5b650|planar graph]] of noncrossing pairings of $2n$ points on two opposite sides of a rectangle with $n$ points on each of the two sides. Concretely, The generator $e_{i}$ is the graph in which the $i$-th and $(i+1)$-th point on the left side are connected to each other, similarly the two points opposite to these on the right side, and all other points are connected to the point directly across the rectangle.
