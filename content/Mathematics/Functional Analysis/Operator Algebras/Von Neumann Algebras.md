> [!definition] Commutant and Bicommutant
> Suppose $\newcommand{\H}{\mathcal{H}}\H$ is some Hilbert space. $A\subset B(\H)$ is a set of bounded operators on $\H$. The *commutant* of $A$, denoted $A'$, is the set $A':=\{T\in B(\H)\mid TS=ST \text{ for all }S \in A\}$. The *bicommutant* of $A$ is the commutant of the commutant, denoted $A'':=(A')'$.

> [!proposition]
> If $A$, $B$ are subsets of $B(\H)$, then $A\subset B$ implies $B'\subset A'$.
> 

*Proof*  For any $T\in B'$, $T$ also commutes with all operators in $A$. $\square$
