> [!definition] Mapping Space
> Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are [[Topological Spaces#^65c94a|topological spaces]], then the set of all continuous maps from $X$ to $Y$ is denoted by $C(X,Y)$ or $\newcommand{\Map}{\mathrm{Map}}\Map(X,Y)$, can be topologized by the *compact-open topology*, whose subbasis is given by the sets 
> $$ M(K,U)=\{f \in \Map(X,Y) \mid f(K) \subset U \}, $$
> where $K\subset X$ is compact, and $U\subset Y$ is open.
>
